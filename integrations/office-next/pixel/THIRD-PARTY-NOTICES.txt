This integration builds the actual upstream Canvas renderer, character state
machine, office layout and bundled assets. It does not run the upstream Claude
hooks, terminal launcher, transcript scanner or persistent settings manager.

Upstream credits the character artwork to JIK-A-4's MetroCity free top-down
character pack. Its author page identifies CC0 1.0 Universal and permits
commercial use. Source verified on 2026-10-01:
https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Bundled furniture/floor/wall assets are taken only from the pinned MIT-licensed
repository. Optional third-party paid packs mentioned in upstream documentation
are not downloaded or included. Separate distribution is subject to a fresh
asset provenance review if additional packs are introduced.
